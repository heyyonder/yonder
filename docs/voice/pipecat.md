# Voice with Pipecat

> [!NOTE]
> Planned. This is not in the app yet.

[Pipecat](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat) is an open-source Python
framework for voice agents. It lets you mix the three steps of a call
freely:

- **Speech to text**, such as Deepgram, Soniox or a local Whisper.
- **The LLM**, such as Claude, GPT or any model on OpenRouter.
- **Text to speech**, such as Cartesia, Deepgram Aura or a local Kokoro.

A Pipecat voice module would give the tools of the bridge to its LLM and
talk to you through the phone. Pipecat adds Python to the install, so it
comes when people need a mix that the realtime models cannot give.

To help build it, open an issue or a discussion first.
