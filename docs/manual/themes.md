# Look and themes

Open **Settings › Appearance › Screen** for the gallery of themes. Tap a
theme to see it live, then keep it or go back.

A theme is one of two kinds:

- **A style** changes the colors, the font, the shapes, the density and
  the words of the default screen.
- **An own screen** replaces the whole screen with a page that an agent
  writes for you.

## Build your own

Tell an agent what you want: "make me a screen with big buttons and a
dark terminal look". The agent builds it on your computer, and the phone
gets each new version in seconds. Your themes are in
`~/.config/yonder/ui/<name>/`.

To share a theme with everyone, read [docs/themes/README.md](../themes/README.md).
