# Trademark

The code of Yonder is under the [MIT license](LICENSE). The brand is not.

The MIT license does not give you the right to use these:

- The name **Yonder**.
- The bird, the app icon and the other icons of Yonder.
- The domain names of Yonder, such as `yonder.so`.

## What you can do

- Fork the code, change it and run it for yourself.
- Say that your project is "based on Yonder" or "a fork of Yonder".
- Use the name Yonder to talk about the project, such as in a review or a
  tutorial.

## What you cannot do

- Publish an app with the name Yonder, or with its bird or its icons, in an
  app store or on a download site.
- Use the name or the icons in a way that makes people think that your
  project is the official Yonder.

A fork that you publish needs its own name, its own icons, its own bundle
ID and its own Apple team ID.

For a question about the brand, write to hello@yonder.so.
